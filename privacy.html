1. Who this applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game 3D Tic-Tac-Toe (package name in.nivetandev.tictactoe3d), published by Nivetan ("we", "us", "our") on the Google Play Store. By installing or using the game, you agree to this policy.

We collect no personal data — none. The game does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any usage or analytics data.

3. How the game works

3D Tic-Tac-Toe is a fully offline, single-player game. Everything — including the game logic and the AI opponent — runs locally on your device. The game does not require an internet connection to play, and it does not send any information to us or to any third party.

  • No ads. The game contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not use any analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools.
  • No third-party services. The game does not integrate Google Play Games Services, social logins, or any other third-party service that would collect data.

4. Permissions

The game does not request any Android permission that grants access to your personal information (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage of your personal files). Any settings or game progress are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

5. Children's privacy

Because the game collects no data of any kind, it is suitable for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Data sharing and security

We do not collect data, so there is no personal data to share, sell, or disclose to anyone. As nothing is transmitted off your device, there is no server-side data that could be exposed.
